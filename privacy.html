Subu Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 24, 2026

Subu is a subscription-tracking and cancellation-assistance app. This policy describes exactly what the app does and doesn't do with your data.

Data storage

Everything you enter into Subu — subscription names, categories, status (Keep/Review/Cancel), notes, and the history of status changes — is stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. None of it is transmitted to us or to any server we operate, because we don't operate one. There is no account, no sign-in, and no Subu backend.

On-device predictions

Subu includes an optional "AI Insights" feature that predicts a cancel-risk score for each subscription. This runs entirely on your device using Apple's Core ML framework. The model was trained offline, before the app shipped, on synthetic example data — not on any user's real data. No information about your subscriptions is sent anywhere to produce these predictions, and nothing about your usage improves or retrains the model.

Advertising

Subu is free to download and supported by ads served through Google's Mobile Ads SDK (AdMob). If you grant permission via Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, ads may be personalized using your device's advertising identifier; if you decline, you'll still see ads, just non-personalized ones. Google's collection and use of data through the Mobile Ads SDK is governed by Google's own privacy policy — we don't have visibility into or control over Google's internal data handling beyond what their policy describes.

Purchases

Subu offers a one-time "Remove Ads" in-app purchase, processed entirely through Apple's StoreKit and the App Store. Subu never sees your payment information — Apple handles the transaction directly.

What we don't do

  • No accounts or sign-in
  • No analytics or crash-reporting SDKs
  • No data sold to third parties
  • No data shared for advertising purposes beyond what Google's Mobile Ads SDK does on its own, per its policy linked above
