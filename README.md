# Subu

Subu helps you discover, organize, and cancel the recurring subscriptions
you're actually paying for — streaming, software, memberships, subscription
boxes, and more.

- **Discover** what you're paying for from a built-in catalog spanning
  Entertainment, Software, AI tools, Cloud storage, Memberships, Gaming,
  Health & Fitness, News, Physical Goods, Consumables, and Social/Creator
  platforms.
- **Organize** every subscription into Keep, Review, or Cancel, with a
  Dashboard overview and smart suggestions.
- **Cancel** with a step-by-step, provider-specific Cancel Assistant.
- **Your data stays on your device.** No accounts, no sign-in, no backend.
