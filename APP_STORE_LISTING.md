## ⚠️ Before you use this

1. **Name check**: "Subu" was chosen specifically to avoid the Sub-Zero
   appliance trademark risk the previous name (SubZero) carried — as a
   coined word, it's a much safer category. Still worth a free, quick
   sanity check before committing to it publicly: search "Subu" in the
   USPTO's TESS trademark database (uspto.gov) — a few minutes, no cost.
   Not something I can verify myself; I don't have live access to
   trademark databases.
2. **Age rating**: the Discover catalog includes OnlyFans and X Premium as
   plain factual entries (name only, no logos, same treatment as every
   other provider like Netflix or Adobe) — this is intentional, since
   they're real recurring charges people track. Apple's age-rating
   questionnaire may still ask about "mature/suggestive content" references
   — answer that honestly based on what's actually in the app (a text
   label in a list, nothing else) rather than me guessing the right answer
   here.

## Description

**Never lose track of a subscription again.**

Subu helps you discover, organize, and cancel the recurring charges
you're actually paying for — streaming, software, memberships, subscription
boxes, and more.

**Discover what you're paying for**
Browse or search a built-in catalog of common subscriptions across
Entertainment, Software, AI tools, Cloud storage, Memberships, Gaming,
Health & Fitness, News, Physical Goods, Consumables, and Social/Creator
platforms. Tap to add — no need to remember exact plan details up front.

**Organize with Keep, Review, or Cancel**
Sort every subscription into one of three simple buckets. See your
breakdown at a glance on the Dashboard, with an overview chart and smart
suggestions pointing out subscriptions worth a second look.

**Actually cancel, step by step**
The Cancel Assistant gives you a real, provider-specific guide to
cancelling — not just "good luck." For providers we don't have specific
steps for, you still get general guidance and a direct link to the
provider's site.

**Optional AI Insights**
Unlock an on-device, privacy-preserving prediction of how likely you are to
cancel each subscription, with plain-language reasoning behind the score —
a one-time purchase that also removes ads.

**Your data stays on your device.** No accounts, no sign-in, no backend.
